My favourite vocaloids

Hatsune Miku ♥

Miku is a female VIRTUAL SINGER known for her blue-green twin-tail hairstyle. She can sing in a wide variety of genres with her cheerful and cute voice. Among VIRTUAL SINGERS, her name is known throughout the world and through many generations.Miku's appearance is modeled after her V3 design, created by the artist iXima. She has turquoise eyes and pigtails that extend down to her shins held with black and red square-shaped clips. She wears a sleeveless, white collared blouse with teal trims. Her tie is teal with a grey tie clip. She has a red '01' tattoo on her left shoulder and wears dark grey detached sleeves with a teal trim that becomes translucent at the top and fan out near the wrists. She wears a grey thigh length skirt with teal trim and thigh-high socks that blend into her shoes, also with teal trims. Her design is mainly inspired by Yamaha synthesizers and keyboards.

KAITO ♥

KAITO (カイト) is a VIRTUAL SINGER. He advises Wonderlands x Showtime in the Wonderland SEKAI.KAITO and the other VIRTUAL SINGERS live in a world called SEKAI, but appear in the real world to consult people who have trouble understanding their true feelings through music.KAITO has blue eyes and short blue hair, with blue-painted fingernails. He has an iconic blue scarf. His outfit design is similar to his V3 package design made by the artist iXima who also did the designs for MEIKO's V3 box art, Luka's V4X box art, the Kagamines' V4X box art and Miku's NT [New Type] and V4X box art. Along with his V3 design being the primary inspiration, elements from his V1 package are also incorporated into the design of his jacket, which borrows the cuff designs, shoulder arrows and blue stripped sleeves.

Kasane Teto ♥

Kasane Teto (重音テト) is a Japanese (and later English) UTAU who was originally created as a troll "VOCALOID" by fans. Her surname 重音 (Kasane) literally means "heavy sound" or "overlapped sound;" while these interpretations are widely accepted among fans, TWINDRILL has not released any announcements about the meaning of the name.Her name テト (Teto) comes from Tetopettenson, a parody song of Le Beau Tambour.Although her original voicebank was only for use in UTAU, many people still mistook her for a VOCALOID (and still mistake her for one to this day). Though she had no final voicebank released for the VOCALOID engine, Teto had a private voicebank with Crypton Future Media, Inc. and there were attempts to release her for VOCALOID in collaboration with AH-Software Co. Ltd.. She later was to be released with a speech voicebank for TALQu in January 2022 and an AI voice database for Synthesizer V Studio in April 2023.

Kagamine Rin & Len ♥

Kagamine Rin (鏡かがみ音ね リン) is a VIRTUAL SINGER. She advises MORE MORE JUMP! in the Stage SEKAI.Rin has blue-green eyes and short yellow hair. Her outfit is very similar to Len's, with a lighter gray pair of shorts and a bow tied around her headphones. Her design is based off of her V2 design, created by KEI, who designed other V2 Vocaloids produced by Crypton Future Media.Kagamine Len (鏡かがみ音ね レン) is a VIRTUAL SINGER. He advises Vivid BAD SQUAD in the Street SEKAI.Len's appearance and looks are modelled after his V2 design made by KEI, who also made the V2 designs for Rin, Miku and Luka. He has blue eyes and short, yellow hair tied back in a ponytail and spiked, designed in a way reminiscent of a bunch of bananas. Len's outfit is very similar to Rin's, with some differences being the tie, a larger shirt, and longer, looser shorts.
